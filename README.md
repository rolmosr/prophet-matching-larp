This paper establishes an improved upper bound on the optimal competitive ratio for the prophet matching problem under adversarial edge arrivals. By constructing a six-vertex, seven-edge instance and deriving exact expressions for both the optimal online policy and the expected offline optimum, the paper obtains an upper bound of **0.41448788**, improving upon the previous bound of 0.418928 established by Soto and Verdugo (2025). To the best of my knowledge, this is the tightest upper bound currently available for this setting. The result is accompanied by a formal certificate in Lean 4.
